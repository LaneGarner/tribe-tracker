How Tribe Tracker works for a school district

Everyone uses the same app. A few people can create challenges for a group — everyone else joins them.

    District Wellness Manager
    Creates challenges for the whole district

🏆 A district challenge
Made by the District Manager → everyone at every school can join.
🏆 A school challenge
Made by a School Lead → only that one school's staff can join.
Rule of thumb: the bigger your role, the more people your challenges reach. And everyone — including the managers — can join in and play.
